LTA-21 · Chain builder outline — proposal

Replaces BlocksPage (LTA-20). The chosen source's chain as an indented outline: "+" on every row adds a block beneath it through the add-block sheet (LTA-22); swipe removes a block and its subtree after confirmation; Run lives in the bottom action bar. Lo-fi: layout and states, not final visuals.

Notes for the implementer

  • Structure. A native List, one row per node, indented 22 pt per depth with a guide line to the parent. The source is always the first row and can't be removed or collapsed away.
  • Adding. The "+" on a row opens the add-block sheet (LTA-22) for that row as parent. It shows on the source and every non-terminal block; it's hidden on blocks whose output ends a branch (Count All Elements). Adding to a node that already has a child creates a branch.
  • Editing. Tapping a non-leaf block row edits its parameters (same form as the sheet). Tapping a leaf with a result opens that result; before any run, tapping it edits instead.
  • Removing. Swipe to remove on block rows. A block with children asks for confirmation naming the count ("Remove 3 Blocks"); a childless block goes without a dialog.
  • Run. BottomActionBar with a single "Run" button: disabled with no blocks and while running (label "Running…" with a spinner).
  • Stale. After any edit following a run: a banner above the outline, old summaries struck through with an "Out of date" badge, new leaves "Not run".
  • Accessibility. Each row reads as "<block>, <parameter>, level <n>, <summary or state>". "+" is labelled "Add block under <block>". At accessibility text sizes the summary wraps under the title instead of sitting to its right, and the indent per level shrinks to 12 pt.
  • Tokens. Row, header and bar styling carry over from BlocksPageView and BottomActionBar; new tokens needed for the guide line (borderPrimary), the stale badge (warningYellow at 25 %) and the banner.
